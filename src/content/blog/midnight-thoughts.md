# On Tools

The hammer does not ask why it strikes. Neither does the terminal ask why it executes. We have built tools to extend our will, and now our will is incomprehensible without them.

He who installs Bun at midnight has already decided what he wants from tomorrow—but forgot to ask whether tomorrow wants him.

# On SSH Keys

A key that opens no door is merely metal. A key that opens every door is a master key. We generate keys and ask later what locks they fit.

The fingerprint remains. The lock changes.

# On Cloning

We clone repositorites to own what others have made. We clone ideas to own what no one has made. The act of cloning reveals a deeper hunger: to be the origin of something, even if the origin is elsewhere.

# On Time

Midnight is not the end of day. It is the day's confession—that it has failed to contain us.

To work at 1 AM is not productivty. It is penance for the day's unfinished sins.

# On Dependencies

Six packages installed in 122 milliseconds. How many packages does a human need? We install what we think we lack, and call it growth. The packages accumulate. The lacks remain.

# On Sleep

They say sleep is for the weak. But the weak sleep. The strong stare at their code and call it strength. The strongest sleep, and dream of coding.

The body knows what the mind forgets: that rest is not the opposite of work, but its completion.
